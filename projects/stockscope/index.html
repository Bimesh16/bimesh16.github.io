The problem

A confident answer needs a traceable source.

Company research mixes reported facts, interpretations, and assumptions. When those layers blur, an attractive summary becomes difficult to verify.

StockScope keeps the evidence close: SEC filing snapshots, reporting periods, source-linked facts, retrieved passages, and a journal for revisiting a research thesis.

The redesigned experience

One workspace. A clear path through research.

Ink-blue navigation, warm paper surfaces, and restrained cobalt accents bring the workspace together. Navigation follows the task, with labeled mobile destinations and company search available through Ctrl/Cmd+K.

  1. Start at the Research desk.

    Resume a report, find a watched company, review recorded changes, or choose the next research question.

  2. Discover and compare.

    Screen locally ingested company facts and compare two or three companies. Each comparison cell retains its source, unit, and reporting date.

  3. Inspect a company.

    Move between overview, financials, filings, and Market & news. Reported history and explicit missing-data states stay visible.

  4. Build a research brief.

    Submit a persistent job in Research studio, follow its stage, cancel it, or return after reload. Reports lead with conclusions and conditional bull/bear cases; detailed research roles can be expanded.

  5. Record, revisit, and learn.

    Keep a thesis horizon and source-linked reviews, watch for filing changes, and work through fifteen original lessons across three Learning lab paths.

Engineering decisions

Make the work recoverable—and reviewable.

Evidence first, interpretation optional

Deterministic evidence briefs are the default. Optional local model interpretations remain labeled and require factual review; a valid citation identifier alone does not prove a claim is correct.

Persist research jobs

A SQL-backed queue stores job state and supports cancellation and reattachment after reload. Queued jobs can resume after a restart; interrupted running jobs are surfaced explicitly without automatically replaying model calls.

Reuse embeddings only when the content matches

Content fingerprints and model-digest collections let repeated indexing skip unchanged passages while invalidating reuse when content or the embedding model changes.

Preserve context in the interface

View, company, and report links survive reload and browser navigation. Comparisons show each reporting period instead of implying that mismatched fiscal periods are equivalent.

Keep thesis history visible

Report-backed theses retain their evidence baseline. Filing alerts and append-only user assessments record how the evidence and reasoning develop over time.

Market & news

Designed for data availability.

The new market workspace includes candle and line views, daily and intraday interval controls, volume and moving-average overlays, completed-bar indicators, keyboard inspection, and source-linked headlines.

The Alpaca IEX adapter and chart behavior have been implemented and tested offline. Live prices, news access, and account entitlements remain unverified. IEX coverage is partial; market-workspace headlines are not yet incorporated into saved research reports.

Current scope

A working foundation with visible limits.

Implemented locally

  • SEC ingestion and source-linked financial facts
  • Fundamental screening and company comparison
  • Hybrid filing retrieval and persistent research jobs
  • Thesis journal, watchlists, and filing alerts
  • Fifteen lessons and persisted learning progress
  • Market workspace and Alpaca IEX adapter

Still outside verified scope

  • Live price/news access and provider entitlements
  • Validated factual reliability of model interpretations
  • Full-market live scanning and streaming
  • Validated backtests or trade execution
  • A public production service

StockScope remains an active local software project. Scheduled SEC refresh runs while the local process is active. AI interpretations require review.
